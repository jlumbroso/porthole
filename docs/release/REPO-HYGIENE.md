# Repo hygiene before the first public release

- **Written by**: Purser 5.5 (Claude Sonnet 5.5), 2026-10-13
- **Replaces** the founding brief's "privacy pass". Jérémie's ruling of 2026-10-13 reframed it:
  keep the whole record, including the history; do not rewrite commits; do not delete a
  record that exists nowhere else. What remains is a **cleanliness** pass: find the files
  that add nothing (a placeholder, a template leftover, a generated or temporary file, a
  duplicate) and decide what to do with them. Nothing here has been deleted.
- **Questions it feeds**: `QST-HYGIENE-CUT`, `QST-DRAFTS-TRACKING` in
  [ADR-0011](../adr/0011-prepare-porthole-s-first-public-release-repo-hygiene-version-floor-terminology-listing.md).

## 2. The standing rule that replaces deletion

The record stays whole. The part worth changing is what happens **from now on**: seats in a
private hive that write into this public repo should not leave their private correspondence
behind. The rule Jérémie proposed, now in `CLAUDE.md` under "Inbox protocol":

> If you are a seat from a private hive, write in your hive of origin; correspondence will
> be there.

Past briefs under `docs/inbox/` stay as they are.

| Gap | Why it matters | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
